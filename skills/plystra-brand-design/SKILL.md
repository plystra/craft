# Plystra brand design

Make the requested identity decision fit the work's audience, medium, and accepted character.

## Scope

Apply this workflow to Plystra work or an explicitly adopted Craft scope: owned projects, sub-brands, and every project under them follow all requirements applicable to their actual media, surfaces, and activities; sponsored projects choose their adoption scope while separate sponsorship admission and affiliation rules still apply. Preserve requirement levels: `must`, `required`, unqualified `never`, and unqualified procedural instructions are requirements; `should`, `recommended`, `prefer`, `avoid`, and `suggested` are recommendations, with reasons recorded for material alternatives; `may`, `optional`, and `can` expressing a choice are options. Lists inherit their introducing statement, while an explicit `must` still creates a requirement. `Should never` is a recommendation; examples and suggested scales add no independent requirements, and recommendations cannot weaken requirements. This focused workflow neither grants affiliation nor establishes full Craft compliance. Stay within the request and existing authorization, respect trusted project guidance and platform instructions, and do not treat this skill as permission to publish or make unrelated changes.

## Choose the material for the deliverable

- Use [brand positioning](references/brand-positioning.md) for purpose, personality, parent and project identity, or the meaning of affiliation.
- Use [identity assets](references/identity-assets.md) for marks, palette, type, layout, imagery, and motion; use [accessible identity](references/accessible-identity.md) for their contrast, movement, and language implications.
- Use [brand language](references/brand-language.md) for names, taglines, and descriptions, and [naming and affiliation](references/naming-and-affiliation.md) for supported ownership and official-status claims.

Inspect the accepted assets, project purpose, and real public contexts before editing. Preserve an accepted identity unless replacing it is the task; a proposed candidate is not an approved master. Sub-brands may have their own personality while keeping honest relationships and accessible expression.

Produce the requested assets or review and inspect them in the intended medium and representative sizes. Explain choices through recognition, legibility, appropriate expression, and the actual project constraints. Use a [decision record](assets/templates/decision-record.md) only when the consequential choice needs a maintained record.

Deliver the assets, the acceptance or candidate status, and the checks performed. Distinguish an authorized sponsorship acknowledgement from ownership or endorsement.
